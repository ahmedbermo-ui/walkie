Walkie privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE]. Walkie is made by [YOUR NAME / COMPANY], [COUNTRY]. Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, a password (stored only as a secure hash by our provider), and the name you type.
  • Your list of people: who you accepted, and who you blocked.
  • Voice clips: the audio you record and send, its length and time, and whether it was delivered and played.
  • Push address: a token from Google Firebase so your phone can be told a new clip arrived.
  • Reports: if you report someone, we store who reported whom and when.

We do not collect your location, your phone contacts, or advertising IDs. Walkie has no ads and no analytics.

Microphone

Walkie only records while you hold the talk button. Recording stops when you let go.

How it is used

Only to deliver your clips to the people you chose, show delivery ticks, and keep the service safe (blocking and reports).

Who can hear your clips

Only you and the person you sent the clip to. Only people you accepted can send you clips.

Where it is stored

On Supabase (database and file storage) in [REGION], and Google Firebase for push messages. Data is encrypted while it travels and while it is stored. Walkie does not use end-to-end encryption.

How long

Voice clips and their audio are deleted automatically after 30 days. Your account stays until you delete it.

Deleting your data

In the app: Menu > Delete my account. Or see delete-account.html.

Children

Walkie is not meant for children under 13. [CHECK AGAINST YOUR PLAY TARGET AUDIENCE SETTING]

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
