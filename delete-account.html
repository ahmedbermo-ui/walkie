Delete your Walkie account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Walkie.
  2. Tap Menu at the top of the screen.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then Delete everything.

Without the app

Send an email to [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Walkie account". We delete it within 7 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address, password, name).
  • Your list of people.
  • All voice clips you sent or received, and their audio files.
  • The push notification address of your phones.

What is kept

Reports you made about other people may be kept, without your name, to keep the service safe. Voice clips are never kept longer than 30 days in any case.
